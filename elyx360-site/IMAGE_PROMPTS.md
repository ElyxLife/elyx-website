## Color Specifications for Image Generation

- **Primary Gold**: #C9A961
- **Dark Background**: #0A0A0A
- **Section Background**: #1A1A1A
- **Accent Light**: #D4B876

## Style Guidelines

- Maintain minimalist, high-tech aesthetic
- Use dark backgrounds with gold accents consistently
- Avoid overly medical imagery (syringes, stethoscopes)
- Prefer abstract, conceptual representations
- Focus on technology, AI, and future-forward concepts
- Keep human elements abstract or silhouetted
- Use clean lines and geometric shapes
- Incorporate data visualization elements

## Recommended Image Sizes

- Hero backgrounds: 3840x2160px (4K)
- Section backgrounds: 2560x1440px
- Blog headers: 1600x900px
- Icons: 512x512px
- Patterns: 1024x1024px (seamless)
- Team photos: 800x800px (square)

## Export Settings

- Format: PNG for transparency, JPEG for photos
- Quality: Maximum (90-100)
- Color space: sRGB
- Optimization: Use TinyPNG or similar for web
