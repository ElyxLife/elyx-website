Our Engineering Culture

🚀 Ship Fast, Learn Faster

We deploy multiple times a day. Every engineer owns their features from concept to production. CI/CD, feature flags, and automated testing are core to how we move.

🤖 AI-Augmented Everything

From code generation to testing to documentation—AI is integrated into every step. We measure productivity by impact, not lines of code written.

📚 Continuous Learning

Weekly deep dives into new AI tools, healthcare research, and longevity science. Learn from medical experts and apply cutting-edge research directly to production.

🌍 Radical Flexibility

Work from anywhere. Async-first communication. Deep work blocks protected. We care about output and impact, not when or where you're sitting.

💬 Radical Transparency

All technical decisions are documented and debated openly. No politics. The best idea wins, regardless of who suggests it.

🎯 Mission-Driven

Every PR is connected to a health outcome. We measure success in lives improved, not just features shipped. Your code has real-world impact.
