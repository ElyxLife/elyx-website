Culture

How We Build Elyx360

Our goal is to study, document and organize the principles, knowledge and processes that underpin high quality long-term care. AI builds the software.

How We Work

High initiative. Low ceremony. A bias toward clarity and action.

PRINCIPLE

First Principles Over Convention

We reason from the ground up. If the conventional answer doesn't hold up to scrutiny, we discard it. Zero politics. The best idea wins, regardless of where it comes from.

PRINCIPLE

AI-Native From the Start

If an AI tool makes us better, we use it. We are building systems where AI handles the mundane so our team can focus on the problems that actually require judgment.

PRINCIPLE

Radical Transparency

All decisions are documented and reasoned. Every workflow, every architectural choice, every tradeoff — written down, visible, and open to challenge.

PRINCIPLE

Minimum Friction to Action

We build lean. The right amount of complexity is the minimum needed. Premature abstraction and over-engineering are as harmful as bugs.

PRINCIPLE

Ownership That Closes Loops

Whoever owns something owns it fully — from design to delivery to outcomes. We don't hand off and forget. We follow through.

PRINCIPLE

Systems That Make Excellence Normal

We don't want great outcomes to depend on heroics. We build systems so that doing the right thing is the path of least resistance.

Want to Build With Us?

We're hiring people who care about getting things right, take ownership seriously, and want their work to matter in the real world.
