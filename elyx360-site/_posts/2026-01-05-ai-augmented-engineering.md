Six months ago, we made a radical decision: every engineer at Elyx 360 would use AI coding agents as their primary development tool. Not just autocomplete—full agentic workflows.

The results exceeded our expectations.

## From Tool to Team Member

Traditional AI coding tools are glorified autocomplete. They suggest the next line of code. That's useful, but not transformative.

We use AI differently:

- **Code generation**: Describe the feature in natural language, get a working prototype
- **Test generation**: AI writes comprehensive test suites with edge cases we'd never think of
- **Code review**: AI identifies security issues, performance bottlenecks, and compliance violations
- **Documentation**: AI generates and maintains documentation automatically

## The Numbers

After six months of AI-augmented development:

- **3x faster feature development**: From weeks to days
- **50% reduction in bugs**: AI catches issues before code review
- **Zero manual documentation debt**: Everything is documented automatically
- **2x more time for deep thinking**: Less boilerplate means more architecture work

## What Doesn't Work

Not everything is perfect. Here's what AI still struggles with:

1. **System-level architecture**: AI is great at implementing features, bad at designing systems
2. **Cross-cutting concerns**: Security models, data flows, and infrastructure decisions still need human oversight
3. **Novel problems**: AI excels at well-trodden paths, struggles with genuinely new challenges

## The Future

We're moving toward a model where engineers are "infrastructure architects" who design guardrails, and AI handles implementation within those boundaries.

This isn't about replacing engineers—it's about **elevating what engineering means**.
