Most healthcare is reactive — you get sick, you see a doctor. Concierge care flips this model: a dedicated team actively manages your health, coordinates specialists, and optimizes outcomes over time. Elyx360 is the platform that makes this possible at scale.

What Elyx360 Does

The platform's capabilities span the full lifecycle of concierge healthcare delivery — from member onboarding to long-term health optimization.

01

Care Pods

Organize members into dedicated care teams with clear ownership. Pod leads, physicians, nutritionists, and coaches collaborate from a shared workspace with accountability dashboards and follow-up triggers.

Pod management, team assignment, ownership tracking, accountability dashboards
02

Risk Engine

Continuously updated risk profiles built from labs, wearables, genetics, and clinical history. Automated alerts flag emerging issues before they escalate, with configurable escalation workflows and safety guardrails.

Risk scoring, alert rules, escalation workflows, threshold configuration
03

Orchestration Hub

Centralized coordination for scheduling, referrals, diagnostics, and logistics. Automated status tracking and communication templates eliminate manual follow-ups and ensure nothing falls through the cracks.

Scheduling engine, referral routing, status tracking, communication templates
04

Personalization Layer

Capture member constraints — schedule, travel, dietary preferences, life context — and automatically adapt care plans. Protocols flex around the member's life, not the other way around.

Constraint capture, adaptive plan builder, unified timeline, context engine
05

Decision Support

AI-powered best-next-action engine that surfaces recommendations with full rationale and supporting evidence. Every decision is logged with traceability — what was recommended, what was chosen, and why.

Best-next-action engine, decision logging, recommendation history, evidence linking
06

Clinical Safety

Safety checks embedded directly into workflows — contraindication detection, drug interaction alerts, and audit trails. Role-based permissions sandbox sensitive data, and every action is traceable.

Contraindication detection, interaction alerts, audit trails, permissions sandboxing
07

Member App

A member-facing interface that surfaces health progress, upcoming actions, and educational content. Members see their goals, track milestones, and become informed participants in their own care journey.

Progress dashboards, goal tracking, health literacy content, milestone notifications

A System That Gets Smarter Over Time

Elyx360 is built as a learning system. Every clinical interaction, care decision, and health outcome feeds back into the platform — compounding institutional knowledge and continuously improving care quality.

Structured Data Capture

Every consultation, lab result, care decision, and outcome is captured as structured data within the workflow — not in disconnected notes or spreadsheets.

→

Cross-Role Context Sharing

The unified member record flows across physicians, concierge staff, and pod leads — within permission boundaries — so context is never lost in handoffs between specialists.

→

AI-Powered Improvement

Outcome data feeds back into the risk engine and decision support system. Recommendations get more precise, protocols get more effective, and the platform scales what works across the entire member base.
