Elyx360

The Operating System for
Proactive Healthcare

Elyx360 is the AI-powered platform that orchestrates care teams, unifies member health records, and automates clinical workflows — powering Elyx, Singapore's first concierge longevity experience.

From Reactive to Proactive Care

Most healthcare is reactive — you get sick, you see a doctor. Proactive care flips this model: continuous monitoring, coordinated teams, and adaptive plans that drive preventive outcomes — care that fits your life, not the other way around. Elyx360 is the platform that makes this possible at scale.

Structured Data Capture

A single longitudinal health profile — labs, wearables, genetics, clinical notes — captured as structured data within the workflow, with role-based access for every team member.

Decision Support

Continuous risk scoring, proactive alerts, and best-next-action recommendations backed by evidence and full decision traceability.

Context Sharing

Automated scheduling, referral routing, and follow-up management across physicians, care staff, and pod leads — within permission boundaries — so context is never lost in handoffs between specialists.

01

Care Pods

Organize members into dedicated care pods with clear team assignment and ownership tracking. Pod leads, physicians, nutritionists, and coaches collaborate from a shared workspace with accountability dashboards and follow-up triggers.

02

Risk Engine

Continuously updated risk scoring built from labs, wearables, genetics, and clinical history. Configurable alert rules and threshold settings flag emerging issues before they escalate, with automated escalation workflows and safety guardrails.

03

Orchestration Hub

A scheduling engine and referral routing hub that centralizes coordination for diagnostics and logistics. Automated status tracking and communication templates eliminate manual follow-ups and ensure nothing falls through the cracks.

04

Dynamic Constraint Mapping

A context engine that captures member constraints — schedule, travel, dietary preferences, life context — and feeds them into an adaptive plan builder. A unified timeline keeps protocols flexing around the member's life, not the other way around.

05

Decision Support

A best-next-action engine that surfaces recommendations with full rationale and evidence linking. Decision logging and recommendation history provide full traceability — what was recommended, what was chosen, and why.

06

Clinical Safety

Contraindication detection, interaction alerts, and audit trails embedded directly into workflows. Permissions sandboxing enforces role-based access to sensitive data, and every action is traceable.

07

Member Connection

A member-facing interface with progress dashboards, goal tracking, and milestone notifications. Health literacy content and upcoming actions keep members informed and engaged as active participants in their own care journey.
