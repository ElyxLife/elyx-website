The Technical Challenge

Healthcare automation is one of the hardest problems in software engineering.

Coordination at Scale

Optimizing human health requires coordinating doctors, nutritionists, trainers, labs, and lifestyle data. Current systems fail because no one can synthesize all the information. We're building AI that can.

Precision Under Regulation

We operate at the intersection of heavy regulation (where lives are at stake) and rapid AI advancement. Every feature must be auditable, traceable, and verifiable—yet ship at velocity.

Automating Automation

To automate healthcare, we must first automate software development itself. You won't just build features—you'll build guardrails and scaffolding to help AI build features.
