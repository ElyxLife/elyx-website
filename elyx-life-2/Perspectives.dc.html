02 — In the Room

Where Elyx Shows Up

Talks, workshops, and activations that connect members and thought leaders across wellness, innovation, and longevity.

The Edition Singapore · Oct 5, 2025
The Elyx Biohack Lounge at CounterClock
Elyx brought precision recovery to Singapore’s most anticipated weekend with an exclusive biohacking experience at The Edition Singapore.
Read More +

During one of Singapore’s most high-profile weekends, Elyx activated The Elyx Biohack Lounge at The Edition Singapore, a red light-illuminated recovery corner featuring spot cryotherapy and neurocognitive performance testing. The signature activation paired red light therapy, which supports cellular energy production, with reaction time assessments that measure neural processing speed and cognitive sharpness. Reaction time serves as a key indicator of brain health and neuromuscular efficiency, both critical factors in maintaining functional capacity as we age. The event drew notable attendees including former footballer Keisuke Honda, DJ Don Diablo, and Netflix’s Bling Empire star Kelly, alongside high-performing individuals drawn to the weekend’s international atmosphere.

Raffles Hotel Arcade · Oct 3, 2025
Train, Test & Track: The F1 Performance Experience
An exclusive F1 Singapore Grand Prix weekend blending elite driver assessments with premium race viewing for YPO members.
Read More +

Elyx partnered with YPO Singapore for an exclusive two-part F1 experience connecting elite driver testing to healthspan optimization. Thirty participants underwent F1-inspired assessments at Elyx’s Raffles Hotel Arcade flagship, including grip strength testing, that revealed critical insights about neuromuscular control and upper body endurance. Grip strength serves as a powerful biomarker for overall muscle integrity and functional capacity, the same strength F1 drivers rely on to maintain precise steering control through high-G corners over the course of a grueling two-hour race. Guests then enjoyed prime race viewing at The Fullerton Bay Hotel in the evening, with views that overlooked the adrenaline-fueled Turn 13 of the night race circuit. Overall, the experience highlighted how performance metrics used in elite motorsport translate directly to longevity markers, while creating exceptional experiences for the Elyx community.

Marina Bay Sands · Oct 1, 2025
Official Biohack Partner at TOKEN2049
Singapore’s first concierge longevity service brought precision recovery to the world’s largest crypto conference.
Read More +

As the Official Biohack Partner for TOKEN2049, Elyx activated a Biohack Lounge on the convention floor, offering crypto pioneers and tech founders access to recovery modalities that optimize performance under pressure. Cryotherapy sessions provided cold exposure for post-exertion recovery, while hyperbaric oxygen (HBOT) and red light therapy offered timed sessions to support recovery protocols for attendees managing intensive schedules. The lounge also featured curated electrolyte protocols designed to support hydration needs. Overall, the activation demonstrated how strategic recovery interventions can sustain peak performance during intensive multi-day events, a core principle of healthspan optimization.
