Medical Command

Precision Care, Physician-Guided

Medical expertise is provided through Elyx Medical, our preferred licensed medical partner.

Dr. Greg Sng

10 years as a primary care doctor with IHH. He is especially interested in how medicine can move towards more personalized, preventive and healthspan focused care supported by the thoughtful use of AI.

Dr. Sureshan Sivananthan
Chief Longevity Officer
View Details +

Dr. Sureshan Sivananthan is Chief Longevity Officer at Elyx Life, Singapore’s first concierge longevity service. A fellowship-trained orthopaedic surgeon whose practice spans joint preservation, arthroplasty, arthroscopy, and metabolic bone conditions, he has authored more than 40 peer-reviewed papers and book chapters and serves on editorial boards including the Bone and Joint Journal and International Orthopaedics. He has worked alongside the Department of Orthopaedic Surgery and Human Performance Lab to study injury prevention and performance, grounding his practice in how the body moves under real demands. At Elyx Life, he guides members through orthopedic concerns proactively, addressing pain, mobility, injury risk, and long-term joint health so members stay active and capable at every age.

Prof. Dean Ho
Scientific Advisor
View Details +

Prof. Dean Ho is Scientific Advisor at Elyx Life, Singapore’s first concierge longevity service. A biomedical engineer and AI-in-medicine pioneer with more than two decades of experience in precision medicine, he serves as Provost’s Chair Professor and Director of the N.1 Institute for Health at the National University of Singapore. He is the creator of CURATE.AI, a first-in-human validated platform for personalized treatment calibration, and recently launched DELTA, a human trial advancing pathways associated with metabolic resilience as a foundation for healthspan. His work has been recognized through fellowships in the National Academy of Inventors and the American Institute of Medical and Biological Engineering. At Elyx, he advises on the development of AI-enabled longevity care grounded in N-of-1 insights and personalized healthspan protocols.

Wellness Command

Holistic Performance, Expert-Driven

Experts, coaches, and performance specialists work in concert to elevate how you feel, move, and live, aligning daily habits with your long-term potential.

Rabia Shah
Head of Movement
View Details +

Rabia Shah is Head of Movement at Elyx Life, Singapore’s first concierge longevity service. A senior physiotherapist trained in the UK and a clinical sleep coach, she bridges clinical rehabilitation with longevity science through a hands-on, evidence-based practice spanning clinical physiotherapy, clinical Pilates, mindfulness, dry needling, therapeutic pain education, and functional rehabilitation, all within a biopsychosocial framework. At Elyx Life, she leads comprehensive assessments of strength, balance, flexibility, and biomechanics to identify early signs of dysfunction associated with injury risk and diminished independence later in life. She designs bespoke programs that support mobility, physical capacity, and resilience, helping members build sustainable movement habits across demanding personal and professional lives.

Annabelle Johnson
Head of Nutrition
View Details +

Annabelle Johnson is Head of Nutrition at Elyx Life, Singapore’s first concierge longevity service. An Accredited Practising Dietitian and member of the Singapore Nutrition and Dietetics Association, she bridges clinical nutrition with longevity science through an evidence-based practice, pairing clinical depth with a genuine love of food to turn the science into personalised guidance. At Elyx Life, she leads the nutrition pillar, assessing dietary intake, body composition, and metabolic markers to catch early signs of risk associated with chronic disease and age-related muscle loss. She designs bespoke nutrition protocols that support metabolic resilience, healthy body composition, and lasting energy, translating each member’s healthspan goals into a personalised nutrition strategy.
