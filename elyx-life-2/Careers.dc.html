Build What’s Next in Healthspan

Elyx is Singapore’s first concierge healthspan service. We bring together physicians, technologists, and experience designers to build the future of healthspan.

Engineering
Head of Engineering+
Location: Singapore · Reports to: COO · Compensation: [base + equity] · Type: Full-time
About Elyx

Elyx is a premium healthspan membership company combining AI-enabled technology and ultra high-end concierge, with multi-disciplinary specialties across science, medicine, physiology, nutrition and supplementation. We exist to maximize client’s healthspan: the physiological foundations for each client to live the life they want, at maximum health.

Elyx places the technology at the center of the membership, with human expertise around it. The product is a continuously-running pipeline that reasons across a client’s biology, life, behavior, and the evolving science of healthspan, and turns that into a stream of predicted, tracked decisions—delivered to the client through the concierge & clients’ app, and, where useful, out to external specialists whose findings flow back in. This is the inverse of most premium offerings in the space, which place a clinic at the center and use technology to support the clinician.

Our work sits at the intersection of science, medicine, software, and clients safety, and serves real clients whose health decisions our technology informs.

The role

We’re hiring a Head of Engineering to own the technology end to end. You will be the senior-most technical leader for Elyx, responsible for architecture, delivery, reliability, security and healthcare-grade compliance, and the engineering team. This role builds the engineering capability that lets Elyx find what works efficiently: high-throughput prototyping, instrumented so the team learns from what clients do, not only from what they say.

You’ll set technical direction, hire and grow the team, and stay hands-on in the hardest technical and architectural decisions. This is a high-ownership role for someone who has built complex, AI- and data-driven systems before and wants to do it in a domain that matters.

The work is held to two standards at once: it has to be sound, and it has to change what a client actually does. Sound means evidence-based and reasoned from mechanism—and, because no two clients are the same, tested against the individual rather than assumed from the population.

Key responsibilities

Own the architecture, delivery, reliability, and security of Elyx. You will collaborate with the product team to understand user needs, set the engineering roadmap, and lead the team of engineers to execute the roadmap in a timely manner. You will also be responsible for building an engineering culture leveraging the changing AI landscape. The core components of the Elyx product are:

  • The reasoning engine. Elyx runs continuously across everything known about a client—labs, biomarkers, wearable streams, imaging, meal photos, self-reports. The engine identifies the real patterns in one client’s noisy data while excluding the false ones, and monitors and validates the evolving scientific literature for the findings relevant to that client.
  • The econometric search engine. This is the module to use the client’s medical and health telemetry to search against the pool of scientific knowledge, biased by the client’s preferences, to generate a list of interventions and diagnostics that should be run to maximize the client’s health. The initial focus will be on improving existing health, but over time, this system will also be useful in helping analyze options for sickcare for our clients.
  • The data model and capture underneath it. The model that turns those varied inputs into one coherent, queryable picture of the client, with a confidence level on each item. Includes capturing the reasoning specialists use when they design a client’s health strategy, in structured form, so the engine can later learn from it.
  • The client app. The surface the client and their concierge work in, and the team’s primary means of learning what works: prototypes wired to record what clients actually do, so the team learns from behavior rather than only from what clients report. The app’s subject is communication—how it asks questions, how it shows progress, the tone it uses.
  • The provider app. The tool the delivery providers and the concierge work in—personal trainers, physiotherapists, nutritionists, coaches, including freelance and overseas providers who pick up a client while they travel. It gives a provider the prepared brief on what to work on for that client and their current state, so a provider new to the client can deliver against the plan without assembling context, and captures the data from each session back into the system.
  • The decision pipeline. When the system proposes a change to a client’s plan, the change carries a prediction of what it should achieve. How rigorously that prediction is measured is negotiated with the client. Once shipped, the change is tracked to its outcome and the result feeds back. This discipline is designed with the specialist providers team.
  • The data and access layer. Confidential health data where access is not a flat on/off list——a concierge, a specialist, and an external partner each see a different, scoped slice, bounded by client consent. Least-privilege access shaped by role and situation.
  • The healthspan coach. Even though the client might know what to do to maximize their health, working through the list of diet, sleep, movement, etc. goals is not easy. We plan to anthropomorphize the AI agent into a healthspan coach to “brain hack” the client into adherence. This is a long term requirement that is part of our vision of where healthspan management will go.
Required skills and experience
  • Engineering leadership, zero-to-one. Proven track record leading engineering teams (10 to 25) as the senior-most technical owner of a complex production platform, responsible for architecture, delivery, reliability.
  • Building the team. Proven track record of recruiting top-tier engineering talent for early-stage startups and building high-performance, motivated teams
  • Hands-on. Actively contributes to the codebase (using AI or otherwise), including code reviews, and builds AI tooling to enhance team productivity; Owns the hardest technical problems directly. This is a building and managing role, not purely managing.
  • Deep knowledge engineering. Demonstrated experience turning unstructured, high-stakes content into structured, queryable, evidence-graded knowledge that professionals act on. Required across two areas: a body of scientific literature, and one subject’s noisy longitudinal data. Plus demonstrated experience structuring messy input at the point of capture.
  • Applied AI / ML systems. Demonstrated experience building and shipping LLM/ML systems in production, with real evaluation, observability, and accuracy and hallucination control.
  • Instrumentation-first. Demonstrated experience building products instrumented to learn from user behavior.
  • Sensitive data access. Built systems where access to sensitive data is governed by role and situation—scoped slices, not a flat permissions scheme.
  • Cloud and infrastructure. Strong modern cloud engineering (AWS or equivalent), CI/CD, and reliability practices.
  • [Strong plus] Clinical data. Hands-on experience ingesting and structuring clinical data—labs, biomarker panels, imaging, EHR and external specialist records (e.g. FHIR / HL7)—into a form a system can reason over, with PHI handling under a real data-governance regime.

About Elyx

Elyx is a premium healthspan company combining AI-enabled technology and high-end concierge, with multidisciplinary specialties across science, medicine, physiology, nutrition, and supplementation. We exist to maximize each member’s healthspan — the physiological foundation to live the life they want, at maximum health.

Elyx Life places technology at the center of the membership, with human expertise built around it. A continuously running pipeline reasons across a member’s biology, life, and behavior — combined with the evolving science of healthspan — to produce a stream of predicted, tracked decisions. These are delivered through the concierge and member app, and, where useful, out to external specialists whose findings flow back in.

Elyx Medical provides the physician-led clinical foundation for this model, including diagnostics, medical review, preventive care, and safety oversight. Elyx Life then helps translate these insights into coordinated, evolving plans aligned with each member’s long-term goals.

About the Role

You’ll own a portfolio of 15–20 clients and be their main point of contact. Your job is to make sure their healthspan plan gets executed — across our clinic, external specialists, and whatever city they’re in that week. You’ll report to the COO, and work daily with our physicians, PTs, and the AI Healthspan Copilot that sits behind every client’s plan.

Your days could look very different; planning out the client’s next month’s sessions, manning the reception, assisting with a VO2max test, setting up a sauna for a client or even accompanying a client to an appointment. The role is very varied but always focused on what progresses a client on their healthspan journey.

We’re still a young startup with a team of ~20 people. You need to be comfortable in a startup environment, where not everything is well documented. You’ll do well here if you relish contributing in many different ways, and see the gaps as opportunities for you to help build the SOPs with lasting impact.

We’re located in Raffles Hotel Arcade. Regular working hours are Mon–Fri 9am–6pm, but you’re expected to handle occasional urgent client situations off hours, including being responsive on chats. Non-urgent matters or messages can wait till the next day.

What you can expect
  • Competitive salary
  • Equity in an early stage start-up with potential for significant upside
  • Coaching and support from experts within the team
  • Competitive health insurance
  • Very little, if any, travel required
  • EP sponsorship if required
Key Responsibilities
Client Relationship Management
  • Deeply understand each client’s healthspan goals, preferences, and lifestyle to provide proactive, personalized service.
  • Build trusted relationships by always putting clients’ interests first and consistently exceeding expectations. Go above and beyond to create delight.
  • Serve as the dedicated point of contact for assigned clients throughout their healthspan journey.
Program Coordination
  • Take ownership of the healthspan journey, collaborating with the care team to continuously enhance service delivery and client experience.
  • Manage and coordinate all scheduling and logistics for client appointments and planned activities—including international arrangements—across providers, specialists, and facilities.
  • Conduct regular check-ins to assess CSAT, track progress and address concerns.
  • Monitor session attendance and wearable trends, flag deviations from the client’s plan, and escalate to the relevant experts in the team for interpretation and action.
Administrative & Compliance
  • Maintain detailed records of client interactions, preferences, and progress to enable highly personalized, proactive service and drive continuous improvement.
  • Handle client health data in line with PDPA and internal privacy standards, and follow clinic SOPs for records, escalation, and scope of role.
Additional Responsibilities
  • Front-of-house duties (including occasional weekends), supporting facility operations and event organization.
  • Build ops SOPs.
  • Actively work with the Product and Tech teams to improve ops efficiency through automation.
Requirements
  • At least five years in high-touch client relationship management or similar role, ideally in five-star hospitality, luxury retail or private banking serving high-net-worth clientele.
  • Exceptional interpersonal and communication skills; proven ability to build and maintain relationships, handling high-profile clients and sensitive situations with discretion and finesse.
  • Excellent spoken and written English. Fluency in Mandarin is a big plus.
  • Excellent problem-solving skills; track record of managing complex scenarios and multiple stakeholders with creative thinking and resourcefulness. Grace under pressure.
  • Bachelor’s degree in a related field or equivalent certifications in hospitality management is a plus.
  • Adept with software and AI tools; quick to learn and adapt to new technology.
  • Keen personal interest in health and fitness.
