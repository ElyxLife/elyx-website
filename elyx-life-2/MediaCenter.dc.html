Elyx Life Launches as Singapore’s First Concierge Longevity Service at Raffles Hotel Arcade

Bringing together performance and recovery with physician guidance and AI-assisted research synthesis to help members maximize their healthspan through personalized, year-long protocols.

Read More

Operating on an appointment-only model, Elyx Life ensures complete privacy and personalized attention, with each member’s arrival coordinated by their dedicated concierge team for a seamless, friction-free experience.

Singapore, 29 January 2026 — Elyx Life, Singapore’s first concierge longevity service, officially opens at Raffles Hotel Arcade, introducing a new approach to healthspan optimization for those who prioritize living better, more fully. Bringing together multi-disciplinary practitioners, an AI Healthspan Co-Pilot, and a research team, Elyx Life designs personalized healthspan journeys tailored to each member’s unique physiology.

Members receive protocols designed and execution support across seven interconnected health pillars: Diagnostics, Nutrition, Sleep, Movement, Mind, Therapies, and Supplementation. Rather than optimizing each area in isolation, these protocols integrate all physiological systems into a coherent whole.

At the center of Elyx’s model is seamless coordination across two layers. First, a dedicated research team works with the AI Healthspan Co-Pilot to evaluate the latest longevity science research, enabling unbiased deep dives that transform exhaustive possibilities into the most impactful strategies for each member. Second, a concierge team coordinates execution across the member’s Circle of Experts, including personal trainers, nutritionists, wellness directors, physiotherapists, podiatrists, psychologists, and nurses — all with prescribed sessions included in the membership. This integration eliminates the friction individuals face when managing disconnected practitioners, conflicting advice, and siloed wellness services. The result: removed coordination burden, increased adherence, and freed mental space for what matters most.

Elyx Life’s performance space features Woodway treadmills, Vald force platforms, and PENT. systems — the pinnacle of training technology trusted by elite athletes worldwide.

“Today, there is no systemic solution to the question: How do I maximize my health?” shares Peng T. Ong, Co-founder and Chair of Elyx Life. “We built Elyx to answer that for each person. We find the latest scientifically rigorous, evidence-based knowledge relevant to members, and help them implement protocols that keep them in the best of health.”

Elyx follows an N-of-1 methodology where no two members follow the same path. The service is built on a structured approach that moves members through four distinct phases annually:

01Comprehensive health decoding to establish physiological baseline*
02Personalized protocol design across seven interconnected health pillars
03Continuous monitoring and quarterly reassessments
04Ongoing calibration as physiology adapts and performance evolves, supporting physician-guided care informed by advanced diagnostics and continuous tracking

*Provided by preferred partner, Elyx Medical

Operating from the Raffles Hotel Arcade, Elyx Life (#02-30) features clinical-grade performance equipment for strength profiling, cardiovascular capacity testing, and functional movement assessments, alongside recovery therapies such as a hyperbaric oxygen chamber, red-light therapy, private Finnish sauna, and cryotherapy.

Adjacent to Elyx Life is Elyx Medical (#02-32), Elyx’s preferred partner. The facility provides comprehensive diagnostics, physician consultations, and advanced imaging in a medically-certified environment, allowing members to move seamlessly between performance optimization and physician-guided care without coordination burden.

“Our approach centers on making health optimization as seamless and intelligent as the best consumer technology — personalized, proactive, and built around how people actually live,” explains Ashish Chordia, Co-founder and CEO of Elyx Life.

Through its partnership with adjacent Elyx Medical, physicians establish physiological baselines and provide parameters that inform protocol execution at Elyx Life. A dedicated concierge team manages all scheduling and coordination, while performance data flows back to physicians for ongoing protocol refinement, creating a continuous feedback loop between medical oversight and lifestyle execution.

L-R: Ashish Chordia, Peng T. Ong, Dr. Varun Reddy, Nishanth Sudharsanam

Elyx is co-founded by serial tech entrepreneurs Peng T. Ong (Chair), who co-founded Monk’s Hill Ventures, and Ashish Chordia (CEO), who founded LG Ads (formerly known as Alphonso). The medical and technology leadership includes Dr. Varun Reddy (Chief Clinical Officer), a clinician-scientist bridging regenerative medicine research with clinical practice; Nishanth Sudharsanam (Chief Technology Officer), founder and former CEO of healthcare data platform Klinify; Cheehan Tee (Chief Operating Officer), who built and led the tech team at logistics company Ninja Van; Arun Jayaraj (Wellness Director), trained in medicine and exercise physiology at King’s College London; and Varun Panjwani (Commercial Director), previously co-founder of healthtech ventures MetaCare, GlobalHealth Asia-Pacific, and Arctic Fitness.

The team is advised by Dr. Dean Ho, Provost’s Chair Professor at the National University of Singapore and founder of CURATE.AI, a first-in-human validated platform for personalized treatment optimization, bringing cutting-edge precision medicine insights to Elyx’s N-of-1 protocol development.

Each membership provides continuous coordination across Singapore and globally, with founding memberships now available for qualified applicants.
